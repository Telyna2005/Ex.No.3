## **Title:**

**Design of an AI-Powered Customer Service Chatbot Using Multiple Prompting Techniques**

---

### **Aim:**

To design an AI-powered chatbot that assists customers in resolving issues related to **product troubleshooting, order tracking, and general inquiries**, ensuring **efficiency**, **accuracy**, and a **user-friendly conversational tone**, by applying diverse prompting methods.

---

## **1. Straightforward Prompting**

### **Technique Description:**

Straightforward prompting involves giving the AI a **clear and direct instruction** without additional context or examples. It is the simplest form of prompting and ensures **concise, goal-focused outputs**.

### **Example Prompt:**

> “Design a chatbot that helps customers troubleshoot electronic devices, track their orders, and answer general questions in a polite and friendly manner.”

### **AI Output Example:**

* **Chatbot Name:** SmartAssist
* **Functions:**

  1. Troubleshooting product issues (diagnostics, solutions)
  2. Tracking orders (real-time shipment updates)
  3. Handling FAQs (return policy, payment issues, warranty)
* **Tone:** Friendly, empathetic, and concise
* **Sample Conversation:**
  **User:** “My device won’t turn on.”
  **Bot:** “I’m sorry to hear that! Can you tell me the model number so I can guide you through some quick checks?”

### **Evaluation:**

✅ Pros: Simple, fast, clear outputs
⚠️ Cons: May lack structure or depth in complex tasks

---

## **2. Tabular Format Prompting**

### **Technique Description:**

Information is structured into a **table format**, helping the model organize content efficiently and enhance readability.

### **Example Prompt:**

> “Present the chatbot’s functions, responses, and benefits in a tabular format.”

| **Feature**             | **Functionality**                                  | **Example Response**                               | **Benefit**                         |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Product Troubleshooting | Diagnose and suggest solutions for device issues   | “Let’s run a quick check to fix your power issue.” | Saves time for customer and support |
| Order Tracking          | Provides real-time shipping updates                | “Your order #4567 is arriving tomorrow.”           | Enhances transparency               |
| General Inquiries       | Answers FAQs about returns, warranty, and payments | “You can return your product within 10 days.”      | Builds customer trust               |

### **Evaluation:**

✅ Pros: Well-organized, clear visual structure
⚠️ Cons: May limit conversational flexibility

---

## **3. Missing Word Prompting**

### **Technique Description:**

This technique leaves **blanks or missing words** for the model to fill in, encouraging contextual understanding and active reasoning.

### **Example Prompt:**

> “The chatbot should help customers with ___, ___, and ___ while maintaining a ___ tone.”

### **AI Completion:**

“The chatbot should help customers with **product troubleshooting**, **order tracking**, and **general inquiries** while maintaining a **friendly and supportive** tone.”

### **Evaluation:**

✅ Pros: Encourages AI reasoning and creativity
⚠️ Cons: Requires clear contextual setup to avoid ambiguity

---

## **4. Preceding Question Prompting**

### **Technique Description:**

This method involves asking a **contextual question before the actual task**, to help the model frame its response logically.

### **Example Prompt:**

> **Q1:** What are the common issues customers face in e-commerce?
> **Q2:** Based on those issues, design a chatbot that resolves them efficiently.

### **AI Output Example:**

**Common Issues Identified:**

* Delayed deliveries
* Defective products
* Unclear return/refund process

**Chatbot Design:**

* **Feature 1:** Real-time order tracking
* **Feature 2:** Automated return/refund requests
* **Feature 3:** Interactive troubleshooting guide
* **Tone:** Helpful and understanding

### **Evaluation:**

✅ Pros: Encourages contextual depth and stepwise reasoning
⚠️ Cons: Slightly longer process compared to direct prompts
